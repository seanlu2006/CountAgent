# Categories

Pick one category per entry. Words in brackets are hints for automatic sorting.

## Spending
- **Dining** — eating out: breakfast, lunch, dinner, coffee, drinks, takeaway, delivery, restaurants
- **Groceries** — food you cook: supermarket, market, meat, eggs, rice, spices (kept apart from Dining so cooking vs eating out can be compared)
- **Transport** — bus, train, metro, taxi, Uber, fuel, parking, tolls, flights
- **Housing** — rent, mortgage, electricity, water, gas, internet, phone, repairs
- **Household** — toilet paper, cleaning, toiletries, small hardware
- **Shopping** — clothes, shoes, electronics, appliances, books, online orders
- **Entertainment** — movies, games, concerts, travel, sport, subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify)
- **Health** — doctor, dentist, medicine, check-ups, supplements
- **Education** — courses, tuition, certifications, study books
- **Gifts** — presents, donations, treating others, family support
- **Other** — anything that fits nowhere else

## Income
- **Salary** — pay, overtime
- **Bonus** — annual bonus, commission
- **Side gig** — freelance, part-time
- **Investment income** — dividends, interest, realised gains
- **Other income** — refunds, cashback, gifts received

## Investments
- **Investment** — regular contributions to funds, stocks, pension top-ups. `type` = investment: neither income nor spending.

## Transfers
- **Transfer** — money moving between your own accounts (cash withdrawal, card repayment, savings). `type` = transfer: neither income nor spending.

---
Add categories whenever you like — just keep the budget table's names matching.
